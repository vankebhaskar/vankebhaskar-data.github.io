Mobile Phone Sales Analysis

An interactive sales analytics project demonstrating revenue analysis, brand performance, monthly trends and product returns.

Dashboard Preview

Business Objective

Key Analysis

  • Monthly revenue trends and year-to-date performance.

  • Product return counts and patterns.
  • Interactive filtering by year, month and brand.
